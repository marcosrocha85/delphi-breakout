A finite campaign

Keep the grid, twin gates, and diamond formations, with seeded classes weighted by formation height. Cheaper bricks favor lower rows; silver and gold favor upper rows and stay separated horizontally and vertically, with diagonals allowed. Follow explicit level transitions with preserved lives and score, an initial 5% speed increase, and a configurable cap.
